# CloudPulse Problem Statement

## The problem

Teams that run websites and APIs often do not know when their service
stops working. They usually find out only when customers complain.

## Who has the problem

- Website owners (for example, an online shop owner)
- Backend developers who maintain APIs
- DevOps engineers who manage servers and deployments

## Why it hurts

- Customers cannot use the service, so the business loses sales.
- Trust is damaged because users see the failure before the team does.
- Developers lose time because they do not know when the outage
  started, how long it lasted, or what caused it.
- Without history, teams cannot see if their service is slowly
  getting worse.

## How people handle it today

- Waiting for customer complaints
- Checking the website by hand
- No record of past outages

## What we want to achieve

A team should know about an outage **before** their customers do,
and have a clear record of what happened.

## Problem statement

> Teams that run websites and APIs often discover outages only after
> customers complain, which causes lost sales, lost trust and slow
> recovery. CloudPulse checks services automatically, records their
> health and notifies the team the moment something fails.
